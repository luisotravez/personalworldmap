# Score your focus

Use this when you have more than 3 "heck yes" items, none, or you are simply not sure which to choose. It takes about 5 minutes.

## Steps

1. Put your candidate items in rows.
2. Score each one from 1 to 5 on three questions: **Ease**, **Impact** and **Energy**. 5 is the best.
3. Add the three scores (the maximum is 15).
4. Sort from highest to lowest.
5. Gut check: does the top 3 feel right? If a lower item keeps tugging at you, that is information. You may adjust **one** score, but only if you can say why.
6. Keep 3 at most. If two items tie, pick the one with the higher Energy. Everything else goes to your parking lot.

## What each score means

| Score | Ease: how easy is it for you to talk about this? | Impact: if you focused on this for the next 6 months, how much could it change your life in a way you want? | Energy: how have you felt after spending time on it before? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | I can talk about it for a long time without notes | It could change my life in a way I really want | I usually finish with more energy than I started with and want to keep going |
| 4 | I can talk about it comfortably with a few notes | It would clearly help | I usually feel good afterwards and it is easy to start again |
| 3 | I can talk for a few minutes, with some notes | It would help somewhat | Neutral. I finish about the same as I started |
| 2 | I would need to prepare before talking about it | It would help a little | I usually have to push myself and feel a bit drained afterwards |
| 1 | I would need to research it before saying anything | Nice to have | It usually drains me or feels like an obligation |

**Energy tip.** Base your number on how you felt the last times you spent time on the topic, not on how you think you should feel. If it is new to you, use a similar topic you have tried, or notice how you feel when you imagine working on it tomorrow.

## Example

| Item | Ease | Impact | Energy | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Topic A | 5 | 3 | 5 | 13 |
| Topic B | 3 | 5 | 4 | 12 |
| Topic C | 4 | 2 | 2 | 8 |

## Where this comes from

This is adapted from ICE scoring (Impact, Confidence, Ease), used by product and growth teams. Here, Energy takes the place of Confidence, and the scale runs from 1 to 5 with the anchors above, because product guides recommend defining what the numbers mean before scoring and warn that a 1 to 10 scale leaves too much room for second-guessing when you have few items. See [sources.md](../sources.md), section 11.
