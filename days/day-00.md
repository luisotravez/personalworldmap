# Day 0: Get set up

## Why this day
Starting is easier when the small decisions are already made. This day sets your tools and your rules, so from day 1 you only have to do the work and publish. You can skip it, or nest it into day 1.

## Steps
1. **Read (6 min).** Three short pieces:
   - Learn in public (swyx): publish what you learn while you learn it.
   - A brief history and ethos of the digital garden (Maggie Appleton): it is fine for pieces to be unfinished, and you can mark how finished they are.
   - The AI Fluency Framework, the 4Ds (optional, if you plan to use an AI).
2. **Choose your tools (8 min).** Decide where you will publish, where you will keep notes and whether you want an AI. See [Choose your tools](../toolkit/choose-your-tools.md).
3. **Set your rules (6 min).** Pick a daily 15 to 20 minute slot. Adopt the [skip rule](../toolkit/skip-rule.md): one skipped day is fine, never skip twice.

## Output
A starter note with four lines: where I publish, where I keep notes, my daily slot and my skip rule.

## If you worry about being seen
You can start an account from scratch, block the people who make you most anxious (you can unblock them later), or post to Close Friends so only a few people see it. The last option will not tell you what to improve. It is an exercise for yourself. More in [Choose your tools](../toolkit/choose-your-tools.md).

## Sources
- swyx, Learn in Public and Learning Gears (base)
- Maggie Appleton, digital gardens (base)
- Anthropic with Dakan and Feller, AI Fluency Framework (base, see caveat in sources.md)
- Lally et al., James Clear, Beshears et al., Sharif and Shu (skip rule)

## Learning curve and supports
Nothing to learn on this day. If you feel stuck choosing tools, take the author's picks (Google Drive for notes, any one place to publish) and change them later.

## Variants
- Nest it into day 1 and do the reading later.
- If you have no time, do only step 2 and write the four lines.

## Close
- What did you decide?
- What still feels uncertain?
- What will you adjust tomorrow?
