# Day 1: Map your interests and pick your focus

## Why this day
Your world can be as wide as you want. To start, you need a focus. Pick up to 3 topics that you want to give most of your attention to during these two weeks. More can appear along the way, but the focus stays at 3 at most.

## Steps
1. **Dump (7 min).** List everything you spend time learning, practicing or creating. 10 to 20 lines. One line each. Do not filter.
2. **Heck yes pass (4 min).** Read your list and mark only the items that make you think "heck yes". If an item is a maybe, it is not a heck yes.
3. **Choose (5 min).** If you have 3 or fewer heck yes items, you are done. If you have more than 3, or none, or you are not sure, use [Score your focus](../toolkit/score-your-focus.md): rate Ease, Impact and Energy from 1 to 5, add them up, gut-check the top 3 and allow one adjustment. Everything else goes to a parking lot. It is not an avoid list. You can come back to it after these two weeks.
4. **Publish (4 min).** Write one line per focus and share them in one short post, in any format. Honest, not polished.

## Output
Your focuses (3 at most), your parking lot and one short post.

## Sources
- Derek Sivers, "Hell yeah or no" (base for the heck yes pass)
- ICE scoring (base for the scoring method, adapted)
- Picsart, how to choose content pillars: start with fewer, because adding later is easier than dropping (support)
- Counterpoint: the 25/5 rule, attributed to Warren Buffett, treats everything below the top 5 as an avoid list. Here we keep a parking lot instead. See sources.md, section 11.

## Learning curve and supports
Expect the dump to be easy and the choosing to feel harder. That is normal. Use the score only if the heck yes pass leaves you stuck.

## Variants
- Voice-note your list instead of writing it.
- Do it with a friend and read each other's lists aloud.

## Close
- What did you try?
- What did you notice?
- What will you adjust tomorrow?
