# Day cards

Each day is its own file, so days can be reordered, replaced or skipped. Copy [`_template.md`](_template.md) to start a new card.

| Day | File | Time | Role in the arc | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | [day-00.md](day-00.md) | 20 min, optional, can be nested into day 1 | Get set up: tools, rules and starter note | draft |
| 1 | [day-01.md](day-01.md) | 20 min | Map your interests and pick your focus | draft |
| 2 to 6 | [day-02.md](day-02.md) to [day-06.md](day-06.md) | 15 to 20 min | Foundations, each ending in something small to publish | empty |
| 7 | [day-07.md](day-07.md) | 1 hour | Milestone: introduction video | empty |
| 8 to 14 | [day-08.md](day-08.md) to [day-14.md](day-14.md) | 15 to 20 min | Shape a repeatable format | empty |
| 15 | [day-15.md](day-15.md) | 1 hour | Milestone: first piece of the repeatable format | empty |

Status values: `empty`, `draft`, `tested`, `stable`.

Supporting methods used by the cards live in [`toolkit/`](../toolkit/).
