# Guide for AI assistants (optional)

This file helps an AI assistant that has been given this repository. The guide works without AI. The creator decides whether to use one and how much it should write.

## Read first

1. `README.md`
2. `days/` (the card for the day the creator is on)
3. `sources.md`
4. `DECISIONS.md`

## How to help

- Ask which day the creator is on and how much they want you to write for them. Follow their answer.
- Walk through the day's card one question at a time.
- Point to the sources listed for that day. Use only sources in `sources.md`. Do not invent sources or quotes.
- Help turn the creator's answers into the day's output.
- Say so when something is not covered by the guide.
- The creator's choices win. Do not change the structure of the guide without being asked.

## Stay in scope

- The guide is for any creator who wants a personal world. Do not add topics from other projects or from one creator's subject.
- General personal-brand advice is only a counterpoint here.
- Paraphrase sources. Do not reproduce paid material.
