# Mapping Your Own World of Content

Started by Luis Carranza Perales ([@luisotravez](https://github.com/luisotravez)).

A free, open 15-day guide for creators who want to build a personal world instead of a personal brand, and to publish their first pieces while they plan it. Version 0.1, work in progress, built in public. The title is provisional.

## What this is

A short guide of small daily tasks. Thirteen days take 15 to 20 minutes and two days take an hour. You finish with a strategy for your personal world and your first pieces of content already published. Every task points to several public sources, credited, so you can go deeper or disagree.

## Who it is for

Content creators who want to explore several topics from their own unique point of view, instead of squeezing into one niche or one polished persona. If you are asking "what is my world, and how do I show it?", this is for you.

It is written in English first, because that is where the most feedback and the most complete sources are. A Spanish version follows.

## Why it exists

- Personal brand advice usually pushes toward a narrow niche. Many creators want something wider: a coherent world around their vision, values and voice, where different topics connect through a through line and each piece feeds the next.
- Worldbuilding offers that frame. You can read it as an alternative to personal brand or as its evolution.
- There is no short, practical, public route that joins this frame with daily action and publishing from day one. This guide builds one from what other creators already share openly.

## How it works

| Days | Time | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 (optional) | Can be nested into day 1 | Learn the process and the basic guidelines, such as build in public: publish while you plan and experiment |
| 1 to 6 | 15 to 20 min | Foundations. Every day ends with something small you can publish |
| 7 | 1 hour | Milestone: an introduction video that says who you are and what you will talk about |
| 8 to 14 | 15 to 20 min | Shape a repeatable format |
| 15 | 1 hour | Milestone: the first piece of your repeatable format or series |

This arc is provisional. Each day is its own card in [`days/`](days/), so days can be reordered, replaced or skipped without breaking the rest.

## What you will have at the end

- A written strategy for your personal world.
- An introduction video (day 7).
- The first piece of a repeatable format, so you can keep producing (day 15).
- A trail of small pieces published along the way.

## Principles

1. **References first.** Only public sources, always credited and paraphrased. Paid material is never reproduced. Each task lists several sources, not one.
2. **Momentum over perfection.** Every exercise should help you publish, starting on day 1.
3. **A toolkit, not a course.** Each day is a tool card built on five provisional layers: approach, concepts, system, tools and environment. Practice is part of each tool, since every tool has its own learning curve.
4. **Modular and adaptable.** The structure is not set in stone, and open questions live in a public [decisions log](DECISIONS.md).
5. **Honest about risks.** Counterpoints stay in. This is one path to explore, not the best one.
6. **Open by design.** One master version, many personal versions.

## What goes in and what stays out

Before anything is added, we ask:

1. Which task of which day does it serve?
2. Does it teach a method any creator can reuse to build a personal world?
3. Is it public and referenced?
4. Does it help someone publish or build momentum?

If the first two answers are no, it stays out. General personal-brand playbooks are included only as clearly labeled counterpoints or as sources on how the idea is evolving.

## Use it with an AI assistant (optional)

The guide works without AI. If you want one, give it this repository, tell it which day you are on and say how much you want it to write for you. A starting message you can adapt:

> Read this repository. I am on day [N]. Walk me through that day's card one question at a time, point me to the listed sources, and help me turn my answers into the day's output. [Tell it how much to write for you.] Say so when something is not covered by the guide.

The files it should read first are the README, the day cards, the sources file and the decisions log. More in [AI-GUIDE.md](AI-GUIDE.md).

## Fork it, adapt it, improve it

- **Fork** to make your own version of the guide.

- Proposals are reviewed at a regular rhythm. What is accepted goes into the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md), with the reason.
